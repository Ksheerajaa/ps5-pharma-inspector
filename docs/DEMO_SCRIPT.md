# Voice-over script: Pharma Inspector demo

Start each line when its cue appears on screen. Each scene stays up until its line is finished, so speak at a normal, relaxed pace.

**1. Dark title card, "Pharma Inspector"**
"Hi, I'm Ksheeraja, and this is Pharma Inspector, for the Bharat AI-SoC Student Challenge, problem statement five."

**2. The app opens on the camera, with a tablet under it**
"Tablet inspection usually needs expensive equipment. I wanted to do it with just a phone, so this runs fully offline on a Nothing Phone 3a."

**3. The orange "Reject: 3 of 4" card appears**
"Put a tray under the phone, and in under a second it finds every tablet and tells you what passes and what doesn't."

**4. The screen scrolls down to the red heatmap on a tablet**
"For each tablet, you can see where the defect is, what kind it is, how serious it is, and how confident the system is."

**5. The "Operator advice" card fills in**
"And here's my favourite part. Llama 3.2 runs right on the phone through ExecuTorch, and explains each decision in plain English: what to do, why, and what to check on the line. The decision itself comes from calibrated rules, and every answer is checked before it's shown."

**6. The amber card with two touching tablets**
"It's careful, too. If tablets are touching and it can't inspect them properly, it won't pass them. It asks the operator to separate them."

**7. "Retake photo" with a glare spot**
"Same with bad photos. Glare, blur or poor lighting, and it asks for a new picture."

**8. The white "All pass" card**
"And when everything's fine, you know straight away."

**9. The "Inspection log" screen**
"Every result is saved on the phone, with the verdict, severity and advice, ready to export for the batch records."

**10. The Bench screen**
"All of this is measured on the phone: an F1 score of 0.95, about 51 milliseconds per tablet, and advice in around five seconds."

**11. The About screen, "Built with Llama"**
"Both models run on the phone's CPU. No GPU, no cloud, no internet."

**12. Closing card, "AI quality control in your pocket"**
"So that's Pharma Inspector: AI quality control that fits in your pocket. Thanks for watching!"
